# 💫 About Me:
Engineering in Computer Technologies student at Tecnológico de Monterrey, with a passion for building software that's functional, inclusive, and meaningful.<br><br>I'm currently interning as a Software Engineer at Iteramind, a startup focused on digitalization for businesses and NGOs — where I'm helping CFOSC, a civil society NGO in Chihuahua, improve their digital presence.<br><br>On the side, I independently develop and publish my own apps. My current project is PakuPaku — a free nutrition tracker designed to be inclusive towards transgender individuals and people with conditions that affect their metabolism.<br><br>I'm also a game developer — my team and I won 1st place at Pixel Borregos' 2025 Game Jam, and we're now expanding that project into a full release on itch.io, with plans for Steam.<br><br>Outside of tech, I'm a lifelong drummer in the metal scene, a campus ambassador for Out for Undergrad advocating for LGBTQ2+ students in engineering and sciences, and a former Scout (2016–2025) — spending my last five years as a section leader across two different groups.<br><br>Always building something. Always learning. 🌱
